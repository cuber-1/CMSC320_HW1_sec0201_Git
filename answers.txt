Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: [For version control and for better team collaboration.]

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to healthcare. For example, hospitals can analyze patient data to predict disease risks and improve treatment decisions.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines the histories of two branches with a merge commit, while rebasing moves a branch's commits onto another base to create a linear history.
